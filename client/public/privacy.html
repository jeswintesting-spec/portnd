Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

At Portnd, we prioritize your privacy and data security above all else. This Privacy Policy outlines how our peer-to-peer sharing application works and details why your data remains entirely in your control.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Portnd does not require user accounts, emails, phone numbers, or passwords. We do not collect, store, or process any personal identification information. When you use our service, you remain completely anonymous.

2. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Architecture

Our application is designed so that files and chat messages move directly between the sender and the receiver's browsers using WebRTC technology:

  • Your files never touch, upload to, or pass through our servers.
  • Your chat messages are sent in real-time directly to the connected peer and are not logged or stored.
  • All transmission data is encrypted end-to-end using WebRTC's native DTLS (Datagram Transport Layer Security).

3. Temporary Connection Signaling

To establish a direct connection between two devices, Portnd uses a signaling server. This server coordinates network handshakes (SDP exchanges and ICE candidates). This coordination data is processed purely in-memory and is immediately discarded once the connection is established or closed. No file content or message text is ever seen or processed by the signaling server.

4. Local Browser Storage

We use your browser's local storage (localStorage) to store simple user preferences locally on your device. This includes:

  • Your chosen display name (default is randomly generated).
  • Your preferred theme setting (light or dark mode).

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache/cookies.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any of your data on our servers, we do not have any database from which to search, export, or delete your information. You are in complete control of your data directly within your own browser.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us at support@portnd.in.
